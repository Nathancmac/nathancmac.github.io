About Me

I am a senior aerospace engineering student at North Carolina State University with minors in astrophysics and mathematics. My academic work focuses on aerodynamics, compressible flow, structures, materials, orbital mechanics, and engineering analysis.

I enjoy using MATLAB, experimental data, and technical writing to connect aerospace theory with real engineering applications. My goal is to pursue aerospace internships and engineering opportunities related to aerodynamics, propulsion, structures, materials, and space systems.

Featured Projects

Converging-Diverging Nozzle Flow Analysis

Analyzed compressible flow through a converging-diverging nozzle using experimental pressure and temperature data. Compared experimental, analytical, and CFD results to study Mach number, choking, and pressure ratio behavior.

Tools: MATLAB, Excel, compressible flow theory, data visualization

Shock Wave Analysis Using Schlieren Imaging

Used Schlieren images to analyze shock wave angles and estimate Mach numbers using oblique shock relations and pressure-based methods.

Tools: MATLAB, image processing, θ-β-M relations, Rayleigh Pitot relation

Composite Laminate Stress Analysis

Evaluated stresses in composite laminate plies under different loading conditions and transformed stresses between global and material coordinates.

Tools: MATLAB, Excel, composite materials theory

Lead Compensator Design for a Pendulum System

Linearized a nonlinear pendulum system and designed a lead compensator using root locus methods to meet settling time and overshoot requirements.

Tools: MATLAB, control systems, transfer functions, root locus
